===== pixel-craft score 6
The revision fixed most of the emblem and icon problems from the first critique. The emblem shading on the lower curve now changes once, the top bar is straight, the forge door is a clean portcullis, the salt-noise speckle has given way to brick courses, the towers that did not mirror are gone, and the 16 px icon now reads as a shield, not a rocket. Exports are still exact nearest-neighbour multiples: 1080p frames are pure 4x blocks, 128 to 1024 are copies of the 64 px master, and alpha is binary on every master.

The hero wordmark has a new, high-severity regression. T and H are built from a left half and then mirrored, and the generator leaves the centre column empty. The outline is then drawn into that gap, so T is cut into two separately shaded halves by a full-height black seam, and the H crossbar is cut in two. AVARNETH reads as "AVARNE-TT-H". The fault is in every lockup, all three variants, the Unity wordmark, flash and shine layers, and every startup frame.

Smaller problems remain in the wordmark. The A counters are still almost closed. The 1:3 diagonals still carry a dot on every stair step: the bright bead is gone, but grey extrusion teeth and mid-tone beads have taken its place. A-R and T-H are still loose. On the emblem, the procedurally drawn mountain relief adds broken ridge pixels and breaks the frame bevel. The A legs on the emblem and the 64 px icon (and the 128 to 1024 copies) have a bead on every step. Icons 24 to 40 show a detached gold crescent under the shield.

Score 6: about 8 for the emblem and icons, held down by the split T in the brand name.

===== brand-design score 6
This revision fixes real problems. The publisher line is now small, flat (#8d92a1), 53% of the wordmark width, and fades in at 0.30-0.60 s to alpha 0.65. The red extrusion line is gone. The A legs no longer merge into the rim. The towers, sky, specks and hanging gem have been removed. The subtitle ramp has been brought down and its ornaments simplified. The icons now come from the same drawing as the emblem. Hierarchy is right: AVARNETH dominates, the subtitle is clearly secondary and the publisher credit is discreet.

The core brand problems are only half solved, though. (1) The new keep sits in the apex of a perfect triangle, and its two side-by-side blue lancets under ear-like merlons read as a face with glowing eyes (owl, robot or hooded watcher). The "eye in the pyramid" idea is still there, now with two eyes. (2) Below the lintel, brick masonry fills the A from leg to leg around a central arched door, so it still reads as a brick pyramid with a tomb entrance, and the glowing striped grille reads as a stove. (3) The wordmark still has no ownable forged detail, and the A counters above the crossbar are still 1 px slits. (4) The revision introduced a new error: the T is split from top to bottom by a black outline column, and the H crossbar is cut in the middle. Both are visible in every lockup and in the startup. (5) The "design directions" are still palette swaps that land on WoW/Hearthstone and Diablo colour schemes. (6) The publisher line is still a generic 3x5 pixel sans that looks like the tool and label text.

Net: better craft and hierarchy, but distinctiveness and concept clarity are still not at the level of a flagship brand mark.
